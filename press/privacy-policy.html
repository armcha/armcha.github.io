Privacy Policy

Hydraulic Press: Crush It (package app.press.game)
Last updated: 13 November 2026

Summary

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob between rounds. Google AdMob collects some data from your device to show and measure these ads (see "Ads" below). A one-time purchase, "Remove ads", turns the ads off; it goes through Google Play. The game itself has no accounts and no analytics, and it does not send your personal data to a server of its own.

Ads (Google AdMob)

If you have not bought "Remove ads", the game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show full-screen ads at breaks in play (after some jobs and after some crushed items). Google collects and shares the following data, sent encrypted, to show ads, to measure them and to prevent fraud:

  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location;
  • how you use the app and the ads (for example, app starts, taps and ad views);
  • diagnostic information (for example, start time, hangs and energy use);
  • device identifiers: the advertising ID and the app set ID of your device.

Google and its partners may place and read cookies or use these identifiers to show ads. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy. The game asks Google for ads suitable for a general audience only.

Your choices

  • Consent. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form before ads can be personalized. You can change your choice at any time with the PRIVACY key in the game's SETTINGS. Where US state privacy laws apply, the same key lets you opt out of the sale or sharing of your data, when Google offers it.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete the advertising ID of your device in the Android settings (Privacy, then Ads).
  • Remove ads. After the purchase the game no longer starts the Google Mobile Ads SDK, so it makes no ad requests and Google AdMob collects nothing through the game.

The "Remove ads" purchase

The purchase is made and paid in Google Play. The game receives from Google Play only whether your Google account owns "Remove ads", and it keeps that answer on your device. The game never sees your payment details; Google handles them under the Google Privacy Policy.

Data on your device

The game keeps its settings, your career stars, the "Remove ads" answer of Google Play, a count of crushed items (it decides when the first ads may show) and a small amount of game state (for example, the settled liquid of the last run) on your device only. This data never leaves your device, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

Network

The game uses the network for the ads and their consent form (Google AdMob), and to ask Google Play about the "Remove ads" purchase. Its fonts and all its other files come with the app.

Permissions

  • Internet: for the ads and the purchase above.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob for the ads above.
  • Google Play billing: for the "Remove ads" purchase.
  • Vibrate: for short haptic effects when something breaks.

Children

The game is made for players aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect data from them.

Older versions

Versions without ads (from 1.3) made no network requests. Versions up to 1.2 downloaded the fonts of the game from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com); like any web request, this showed your IP address to Google, and those versions sent no other data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
