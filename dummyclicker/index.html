DUMMYCLICKER

Two small prototypes. Both are portrait, one thumb, no menus — you tap once and you are playing. They are meant for the moment you are tired and just want something to do. Best on a phone.

ICY HOPPER
Climb the ice, never stop
You bounce upward by yourself, so the only thing you do is steer. Tap a side, or drag your thumb and the climber follows it; hold still to go straight up. Aim for the holes in the floors. A floor with no hole you break with your head. Snow rises from below and it is the only thing that kills you — the birds and the seals just knock you down. Purple veggies build your kit: boots, then a pick, then a fur coat.
TAP A SIDE, OR DRAG
IGLOO STACKER
One tap, one block
An ice block slides side to side. Tap to drop it on the tower. The part that hangs over the edge falls off, and the next block is that much smaller. A perfect drop grows it back.
TAP ANYWHERE
